Overview

  • Managed a team of ~10 people to design a low-cost, open source benthic lander that collects sediment, water, DNA, and video from ocean trench depths of over 9000m.
  • Led the mechanical design, which includes a custom soil scoop, Niskin tube, and a pressure-powered DNA collector.
  • Completed multiple successful tests in SF and in Monterey Bay in collaboration with Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute (MBARI).
