Overview

  • Redesigned an L3 rocket payload that synthesizes crystals in microgravity for pharmaceutical testing to compete in the International Rocket Engineering Competition (IREC).
  • Engineered a rigid, compact payload that fit within a 1U cubesat utilizing a combination of laser cut metal and 3D printed interiors.
